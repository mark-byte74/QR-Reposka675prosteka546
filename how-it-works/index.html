How it works

From paper menu to digital in four steps

We handle the full technical setup; you provide the content and then run your menu independently

  1. 01

    We receive your restaurant's content

    You send us your items, prices, photos, logo, and colors, and we enter everything into your own system at once

  2. 02

    We prepare your identity and codes

    We set up the menu with your restaurant's name, logo, and colors, and generate a dedicated QR code for each table from the studio, ready to print

  3. 03

    You place the codes on the tables

    Print the codes and place them on tables or at the entrance. Guests scan with their phone camera and the menu opens instantly, no app needed

  4. 04

    You run your menu independently

    From your own admin panel, change prices, add items, disable what ran out, and add a new category. Every change appears for guests instantly

A technical note

The guest menu works with no app and no account, and opens even on weak internet, while the admin panel is protected by your own login
