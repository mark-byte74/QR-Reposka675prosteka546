Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the two forms on this site collect and how that information is used. It applies only to information submitted through the partners application form and the contact form.

Last updated: November 2026

  1. Information We Collect
  2. How We Use It
  3. Sharing and Disclosure
  4. Data Security
  5. Data Retention
  6. Your Rights
  7. Contact Us

Information We Collect

The site collects only what visitors submit through its two forms. The partners application form collects your name, company, business email, country, business type, business network, website, and any additional information you choose to include. The contact form collects your name, phone number, business name, and message. Nothing else is collected by the site.

How We Use It

The information you submit is used only to review your submission and reply to it. We do not use it for marketing unless you have given your consent.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties. The only exception is the email-delivery service that transmits your submission — Google Apps Script and Gmail — which receives it only as needed to deliver it to us.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information submitted through the site. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep submitted information only as long as needed to handle the enquiry it relates to. Once it is no longer needed, it is deleted.

Your Rights

You can ask for a copy of the information you submitted, or ask us to delete it. Send your request to the email address shown on the contact page and we will handle it.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, or about the information you submitted, reach us through the contact page or at the email address shown on the contact page.
