Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit a form on this website, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, business name, and the contents of your message. We also collect basic technical information that your browser shares automatically, such as the type of device and browser you use

How We Use It

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare your quote, and communicate with you about our services. We do not use your information for any purpose unrelated to your enquiry without your consent

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing. We may share it only where necessary to provide the service you requested, or where required by law

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure

Data Retention

We keep the information you provide only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting business relationship, and then delete it

Your Rights

You may ask us to correct, update, or delete the personal information you have shared with us. To make such a request, contact us using the details on the Contact page

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please contact us through the Contact page
