What Your Clients Receive

When you introduce MenuCodes, this is what the hospitality businesses in your network receive.

01

A Menu in Their Own Identity

Every venue gets its menu in its own name, logo, colours, and fonts, in Arabic and English — opened with one scan, no app to download.

02

QR Codes for Every Table

A dedicated QR code for each table, generated in the venue's identity and ready to print — guests scan and the menu opens instantly.

03

One Admin Panel, Full Control

Prices, items, photos, availability, and categories — the venue manages everything from one admin panel, and changes appear for guests instantly.

04

Offers and Contact, Built In

Today's offers and promotions, plus a contact card with phone, WhatsApp, maps, and working hours — all inside the menu.
