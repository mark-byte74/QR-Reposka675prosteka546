Problems we know, with solutions built into the system

Six situations restaurant and café owners describe again and again, and how Menu Codes handles each one in practice

Every time I change a price I have to print a new menu, while the old copies are still on the tables

Instant updates from the admin panel. Any change to a price, item, or photo appears for guests immediately, with no printing and no outdated copies

An item runs out in the kitchen but it's still on the menu, so the guest picks it and we have to apologize

One-tap item management: disable the item that ran out or hide it automatically from the guest menu instead of showing it crossed out

Tourists can't read the Arabic menu, so they ask the waiter about every dish or just order the cheapest

An AR/EN button flips the entire menu to English, with full support for both directions, and the system remembers each guest's choice

Generic QR codes look ugly and nothing like my restaurant, and any custom design costs me a designer

A QR studio in your restaurant’s identity: eight styles in your colors with your logo, with live preview and print-quality downloads

I don't know which items guests actually look at, so I make changes by guessing

Analytics inside the admin panel: views, most-viewed items, QR scans per table, and language and device breakdowns

Guests are asked to download an app to see the menu, so they ignore it and ask for the paper one

A single scan with the phone camera opens the menu instantly. No app and no sign-up, even on weak internet
