Pricing

Pricing tailored to your business

No price list and no packages to choose from. Every quote is built on your business alone: your menu, your identity, and what your guests actually need

Why we price this way

Tailored to your business

Every venue is different, restaurant or café: menu size, number of items, required languages, and identity details. So your quote is built on your actual needs: the items, photos, and categories going into your menu, the languages your guests use, and your full identity from logo to colors

Complete transparency

That way you never pay for what you don't need, and your menu gets everything it deserves. And because the system is complete from the start, your quote never hides a second invoice: the menu, the table codes, the admin panel, and the full setup in one conversation

Grows with you

And when your menu grows later, the system grows with it: new items and categories join what you already have, with no rebuild and no migration

What the system includes

Every quote covers the full system. No lite versions, no locked features

01

The guest menu in your identity

A complete QR menu carrying your business's name, logo, and colors, in Arabic and English, opened with one scan and no app

A dedicated code for every table
