Features

One system for everything your menu needs

Thirty documented features, designed from real hospitality work: a clearer experience for guests, and easier management for you

The guest experience

What your guest sees from the moment of the scan to the moment they choose a dish

Reaching the menu

01

QR menu by phone scan, no app

Guests scan the code with their phone camera and the menu opens instantly. No app download and no account

02

Arabic/English one-tap toggle

An AR/EN button flips the entire menu between both languages with full bidirectional support, and the system remembers each guest's choice

03

Installable as an app

An install button adds the menu as an icon on the phone screen. One-tap access for regular guests

04

Works on weak internet

A static, cacheable version opens even on a weak connection. No waiting and no apologies

Content that sells the dish

05

A photo for every item

An optimized photo for every dish, shown on the card and full-size on the item page, because guests eat with their eyes first

06

Descriptions in Arabic and English

A written description for every item in both languages that explains the dish. No cryptic names and no guessing

07

The dish's story

A paragraph telling each dish's story. A detail that builds connection with the dish and justifies its price (can be enabled in settings)

08

Ingredients list

Each dish's ingredients shown with clear badges, for guests with allergies and diets, no need to ask the waiter

09

Dietary tags (vegetarian, gluten-free...)

Standard badges on every item. A growing segment of guests finds itself in your menu

10

Calorie counts

Calorie counts shown for every item. Information health-conscious guests expect

11

Prep-time display

Expected prep minutes shown for every item, so guests in a hurry know what to pick

12

Spice-level indicator

A visible one-to-three indicator of heat level. No surprises and no angry reviews

13

Item badges

Most ordered, chef's pick, signature dish, seasonal. Your standout dishes never get lost among dozens

14

“Pairs well with” suggestions

A section suggesting accompanying dishes for each item. Extra sales happen automatically without the waiter stepping in

Easy menu navigation

15

Categories with icons and carousels

Category tabs with icons and item counts, plus featured, most-ordered, and seasonal carousels. A long menu stays easy to browse

16

Three menu views

Guests switch between grid, list, and compact views, because one layout doesn't suit everyone

17

Complete contact card

Phone, a WhatsApp button with a ready message, maps link, website, and working hours: all inside the menu

Restaurant management

Everything you need to run your menu daily, from one place

Daily updates

18

Instant updates from the admin panel

Any change to a price, item, photo, or availability appears for guests instantly. No printing and no waiting

19

Full item management

Add, edit, and delete items, toggle availability with one tap, and control display priority

20

Category management

Create and edit menu categories with their icons from the panel. A new category doesn't mean a redesign

21

Bulk menu import

Import your entire menu at once through the import interface: a fast launch instead of manual entry that takes days

Identity and codes

22

Full white-label identity

Your restaurant’s name, logo, colors, and fonts everywhere. The menu looks like you made it yourself

23

Branded QR studio

Generate codes in your restaurant's colors and identity: eight styles with live preview and print-quality SVG and PNG downloads

24

A dedicated QR code per table

A code for each table that shows its number in the menu, so you know which tables engage the most

Data and decisions

25

In-admin analytics

Views, QR scans per table, most-viewed items, and language and device breakdowns. Decisions built on numbers, not guesses

26

Item view counter

A view counter for every item, so you know which dish actually catches the eye before ordering (can be enabled in settings)

Settings and security

27

Comprehensive owner settings

WhatsApp number, address, daily working hours, social links, location, and currency, all managed from one place

28

Optional price hiding

An option to hide prices from the guest menu, for restaurants that prefer not to show them publicly

29

Hide unavailable items

Automatic hiding of unavailable items, instead of showing them crossed out with an impression of poor management

30

Security and access control

Protected login, password changes, recovery codes, and session sign-out, with documented security auditing
